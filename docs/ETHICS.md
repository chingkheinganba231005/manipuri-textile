# Ethics and cultural responsibility

Computational work on living textile traditions can support weavers — documentation, design
exploration, faster drafting — but it can also enable misappropriation. This project follows
these principles.

1. **Community first.** Collect data only with informed consent (see
   [DATA_COLLECTION.md](DATA_COLLECTION.md)); involve weavers and textile experts in evaluating the
   outputs; credit makers and sources; share results (designs, weaving booklets, the demo) back
   with the communities.
2. **Geographical Indications.** *Moirang Phee* is reported to be a registered Geographical
   Indication (GI) of India, together with other Manipuri textiles. A GI protects the *name* and the
   origin of the product. Generated designs must not be sold or advertised as "Moirang Phee" (or any
   GI-registered name) unless produced by authorised producers. Check the current GI register before
   publication.
3. **No authenticity claims without evidence.** The procedural grammar and the colour presets are
   approximations made for pretraining; they are not authenticated designs. Claims that generated
   designs are "authentic" or "traditional" require evaluation by weavers / experts and real data.
4. **Attribution in the demo.** The demo states that generated designs are for research,
   education and inspiration, credits Manipuri weavers and points to this document.
5. **Data minimisation and control.** Publish design grids rather than photographs when owners
   prefer; never publish personal data of makers without consent; honour withdrawal requests in
   future releases.
6. **Benefit, not replacement.** The intended users are weavers and designers (drafting help,
   loom-ready instructions, preservation of motifs), not mass-production of imitation textiles.

Questions to discuss in the paper: who owns a design generated from community patterns; how
benefit sharing could work (e.g. weaver cooperatives co-hosting the tool); risks of homogenising
regional variation; the gap between a grid design and the tacit knowledge of a weaver (yarn,
tension, finishing) that the system does not capture.
